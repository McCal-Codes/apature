# External references (looked at, 2026-10-14)

Official pages viewed in the browser and captured to a private reference
board (kept out of the repo: the images are other people's work). What
each one teaches, in the order worth studying.

| who | where | what to take |
|---|---|---|
| Firefox Kit | https://www.firefox.com/en-US/kit/ · https://blog.mozilla.org/en/firefox/meet-kit/ | soft gradient body from the logo colours on a deep purple ground; **no mouth**; eyes with a coloured catchlight, ears and the tail do the acting; a published front / three-quarter / side turnaround; appears at onboarding, discovery and small wins "then steps back"; pencil first, Illustrator after; agency JKR, illustrator Marco Palmieri |
| Duolingo Duo | https://blog.duolingo.com/reshaping-duo/ · https://blog.duolingo.com/shape-language-duolingos-art-style/ | thirty-six silhouettes before a single face; species and colour fixed up front; everything built from rounded rectangle, circle and rounded triangle; "pointy shapes are off-brand" |
| Krita Kiki | https://krita.org/en/about/mascot/ | a turnaround and a licence on one page; splash-screen-only presence since 2.8; "no definitive design", fan art invited; GPL + CC BY-SA |
| Rust Ferris | https://rustacean.net/ | six poses (original, flat, extra-cute, happy, gesturing, plus Corro) as PNG and SVG; CC0; "unofficial mascot" kept outside the trademark policy |
| Go gopher | https://go.dev/blog/gopher | hand-drawn line, three-view strip; CC BY 4.0; "a character, not the logo" |
| GitHub Octodex | https://octodex.github.com/ | one base body in 160+ costumes; a registered mark that may not be used as another app's icon |
| Minecraft Blub | https://minecraft.wiki/w/Blub | palm-sized, blocky, softly glowing, one-pixel eyes, jiggles |
| Nikon-chan | https://petapixel.com/2026/08/24/nikon-is-selling-an-adorable-plushie-keychain-of-its-mascot/ | a camera maker with an official cute character (Japan only), wearing the camera as a prop |
| Discord Wumpus / Clyde | https://discord.com/branding | rendered 3D character; the logo face has no mouth; two characters with different jobs |
| Miffy | https://www.miffy.com/ | dots for eyes, a cross for a mouth, front-facing, a fixed small palette |
| Line Friends Brown | https://linefriends.com/en | an expressionless face works when the context supplies the emotion |
| Kumamon | https://kumamon-land.jp/ | black body, two red cheeks, a constant look of surprise; one accent colour |
| Hootsuite Owly, Tripadvisor owl | https://www.hootsuite.com/ · https://tripadvisor.mediaroom.com/logo-guidelines | the two owl logos any new owl will be compared with (plus Duolingo) |

Not reachable from the sandbox browser: lucasthespider.com (denied),
YouTube (blank), Android brand page (text only), Sanrio (pop-up).

What the board says, read together: the characters people love are
drawn (pencil, soft shading or a confident line), not assembled; the face
is reduced to eyes and at most a beak or cross; one detail carries the
emotion; the body is one or two colours plus a cheek; and every one of
them was published with a turnaround and a licence on the same page.
