Apature brand · v0.3 · 2026-10-14 · character: Fen, a fennec fox (placeholder art; an illustrator will draw the final)

Big ears, quiet eyes.

Apature is a photojournalist's tool: cull, caption, ingest, deliver, on deadline. The interface is dark, dense and quiet. The brand is the one place it looks up at you, and that look belongs to Fen.

Fen is a fennec fox drawn like a storybook: a thin ink line, flat pastel fills, dot eyes with a catchlight, a tiny nose and mouth, blush. Head-on, the head is the mark and the app icon. Sitting, waving or asleep, Fen is the character for the empty state, the finished job and the README. Natural, never creepy: dark eyes with a highlight, rounded ears, real-animal expressions.

Why a fennec

The ears are the identity: the biggest ears relative to body size of any canid, on a nocturnal desert animal that listens for what it cannot see. They are also what keeps Fen its own next to Firefox's Kit. "Fennec" was Mozilla's old codename for Firefox on Android, so the character is Fen, never "Fennec". The research is in docs/design/MASCOT_RESEARCH.md; the brief for the illustrator is docs/design/ARTIST_BRIEF.md.

Two palettes, one character

Inside the app Fen wears the Apature set (lavender-cream body, charcoal line) on the darkroom ground. On marketing surfaces, the README and stickers, Fen wears the warm set (apricot, cream, pink inner ear) on peach or white. Same drawing, two colourways; never mix them in one surface.

Guidelines

Voice & tone

Apature talks like a good photo editor on deadline: direct, specific, calm. It says what happened and what to do next. It never apologises in a dialog, never jokes while a card is copying, and never hides a failure behind a friendly word.

Say what happened, with numbers."Every file verified at every destination · 242 MB in 2.2 s."
Name the next action."2 files failed. The card has not been changed; fix the destination and ingest again."
Let Fen carry the warmth.The copy stays plain; the character supplies the look.
Desk words are welcome.-30- on the done toast, TK on the empty state, "Filed." after export.
No "Oops!", "Yay!" or exclamation marks in the app.Fen can look surprised; the text cannot.
No mascot or motion on the contact sheet.Culling is the work; nothing competes with the pictures.
Guidelines

Visual elements

One design system, two appearances. Apature Dark is a professional darkroom: deep charcoal, compact controls, lavender highlights. Apature Light is a Mac studio: off-white, system blue, open spacing. Lavender is not arbitrary: lavender oil was the solvent in Niépce's first photographs (1826–27).

Proportions and line

Head wider than tall, ears about as tall as the head, rounded tips. Eye line below the head's midline; eyes are dots with one catchlight; the mouth is a small "w", never a grin. Ink line 4.5 units on a 512 canvas (about 1 pt at 112 px), round joins and caps, slightly wobbly on purpose; fills are flat, no gradients. Clear space one ear-width on every side; the mark never below 16 pt, a pose never below 48 pt.

Guidelines

Motion rules

From docs/design/MOTION_DESIGN.md and the Firefox Kit source read-through: Firefox bakes each Kit animation into the asset and plays it once at an earned moment; a looping idle beside other content fails WCAG 2.2.2.

One-shot only.Any animation finishes within 5 s and never loops.
User events only.Job done, ingest finished, error, empty folder, first run. Fen never fidgets while you work.
Never on the working surface.No motion in the sheet, preview, caption or metadata panes.
Reduce Motion.Static frame, or an opacity or colour change only.
Ears first.The ears and the eyes carry the acting; the tail second. Eye darts of one or two frames at a change of attention.
Springs, bounce ≤ 0.3.Interruptible and retargetable; squash at most 12 %.
A switch."Show Fen" in Settings, honoured everywhere.
Baked set pieces.First-launch peek and the finished-job celebration ship as frames, like Kit's.
Library

Logo library

The mark is Fen's head. Use the app colourway on both app grounds and the warm colourway on marketing surfaces; the mono versions are for one-colour print, engraving and the menu bar. The app icon puts the head on the charcoal squircle. The brush-stroke alternates are for stickers and the README, warm on white only.

Wordmark
