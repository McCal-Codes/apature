Apature brand · v0.2 · 2026-10-14 · character: Dek (provisional)

Fast hands, quiet eyes.

Apature is a photojournalist's tool: cull, caption, ingest, deliver, on deadline. The interface is dark, dense and quiet. The brand is the one place it looks up and blinks, and that look has a name: Dek, an owl.

Dek is drawn the way Firefox's Kit is drawn: a soft body, dark eyes with a lavender catchlight, no mouth, and two details that do all the acting, the ear tufts and the wings. Head-on and still, Dek is the mark and the app icon. In a pose, Dek is the mascot for the empty state, the finished job and the README.

Why an owl, and why this owl

Owls see in the dark, cannot move their eyes and turn their heads instead, and have a rod-dominated retina: the darkroom animal. Owls are also the most crowded animal in software (Duolingo, Hootsuite, Tripadvisor), so Dek is built to be unlike those three: a charcoal body, eyes set low with a lavender catchlight instead of ring-eye emblems, no mouth, soft shading on a dark ground. The full research is in docs/design/MASCOT_RESEARCH.md.

Name

"Dek" is newsroom shorthand for the line under a headline, spelled wrong on purpose so it stands out in copy, like hed and lede. It checked clear in software; "Lumen" did not (three photography apps). Alternatives that also checked clear: Cutline, Lede.

Guidelines

Voice & tone

Apature talks like a good photo editor on deadline: direct, specific, calm. It says what happened and what to do next. It never apologises in a dialog, never jokes while a card is copying, and never hides a failure behind a friendly word.

Say what happened, with numbers."Every file verified at every destination · 242 MB in 2.2 s."
Name the next action."2 files failed. The card has not been changed; fix the destination and ingest again."
Let Dek carry the warmth.The copy stays plain; the character supplies the look.
Desk words are welcome.-30- on the done toast, TK on the empty state, "Filed." after export.
No "Oops!", "Yay!" or exclamation marks in the app.Dek can look surprised; the text cannot.
No mascot or motion on the contact sheet.Culling is the work; nothing competes with the pictures.
Guidelines

Visual elements

One design system, two appearances. Apature Dark is a professional darkroom: deep charcoal, compact controls, lavender highlights. Apature Light is a Mac studio: off-white, system blue, open spacing. Dek keeps the dark body in both. Lavender is not arbitrary: lavender oil was the solvent in Niépce's first photographs (1826–27), so the accent is photography's origin.

Proportions

Head and body are one rounded form with no neck. The eye line sits at 46 % of the body height, below the midline, as the baby-schema studies say. Both eyes carry one catchlight at the same clock position. Gap between the eyes at most one eye width. Tufts and wings are the only parts that move between poses; the beak is fixed; there is no mouth. Clear space is one eye width on every side; the mark is never smaller than 16 pt, a pose never smaller than 48 pt.

Guidelines

Motion rules

A looping idle animation beside other content fails WCAG 2.2.2 at Level A, and Apple's Reduce Motion guidance says decorative motion should stop entirely. So:

One-shot only.Any animation finishes within 5 s and never loops.
User events only.Job done, ingest finished, error, empty folder, first run. Dek never fidgets while you work.
Never on the working surface.No motion in the sheet, preview, caption or metadata panes.
Reduce Motion.Static frame, or an opacity or colour change only.
Blink, if at all.Empty state and About only: 100–150 ms, randomised 3–6 s, stops after 5 s.
Small travel.Squash and stretch at most 5 %; nothing in the periphery.
A switch."Show Dek" in Settings, honoured everywhere.
Pre-baked frames.No animation runtime until measured against decode load.
Library

Logo library

The mark is Dek head-on with no feet or wings. Use the dark-body mark on both grounds; the mono versions are for one-colour print, engraving and the menu bar. The app icon puts Dek on the charcoal squircle with the lavender glow.

Wordmark

"Apature" set in SF Pro Display Semibold, tracking −2 %. The SVGs reference the system font; outline the text before sending the wordmark anywhere SF is not installed.
