Rationale

Follows the macOS skeleton exactly: source-list sidebar, unified toolbar, thin dividers, system appearance (light shown). Identity is confined to the lavender focus and selection and the serif assignment headline.

Wins

  • Fastest path to HIG and VoiceOver compliance
  • Discoverable: every command visible in menus and toolbar
  • Cheapest to build in egui

Trade-offs

  • Light chrome and a mid-grey photo ground compete with the image
  • Least distinctive; could be any Mac app
  • Density is comfortable, not deadline-fast

Rationale

Everything near a photograph is hue-neutral dark grey; the accent is desaturated lavender and only ever marks focus. Mono uppercase metadata, numbered class chips, a 6-across sheet and a filter bar built for a deadline.

Wins

  • Best photographic evaluation surface
  • Highest useful density; filenames and marks legible at 6 across
  • Carries the mccal.dev type hierarchy without its tint

Trade-offs

  • Needs a designed light theme for daylight laptops
  • 11 px mono must be checked on 1x displays and with VoiceOver
  • Dense toolbars tempt feature creep; the tool-visibility setting from direction 3 keeps it honest

Rationale

The grid is the window. Sidebar and inspector live on the edges and open on hover, key or VoiceOver command; one floating toolbar; tools appear only when enabled, Folio-style. Chrome uses the mccal.dev tokens directly.

Wins

  • Calmest, most distinctive; the photograph has the whole window
  • Customisation is the product idea, not a settings page

Trade-offs

  • Discoverability and VoiceOver navigation of transient panels are hard problems
  • Risks hiding file-operation status, which the brief forbids
  • Hover edges do not exist for keyboard-only users; needs duplicated commands

Recommendation: direction 2's surfaces and density on direction 1's macOS skeleton, with direction 3's tool visibility as a setting. Rationale and sources in docs/design/DESIGN_RESEARCH.md.
